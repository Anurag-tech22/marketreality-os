# 2-Minute Demo

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook

"CoinMarketCap can tell me the market has billions in volume. But how much of that market am I actually seeing?"

## 0:15–0:35 — Reality Audit

Enter BTC or SOL.

Show:

- current quote
- observed market pairs
- venue concentration
- volume concentration
- coverage
- freshness

## 0:35–0:55 — Evidence

Open one finding.

Show:

source endpoint → timestamp → raw observation → calculation → result → limitation.

## 0:55–1:15 — Trade Reality

Enter:

$100,000 BUY

Show structural capacity and evidence.

Say:

"This is not an execution quote. It is a structural assessment of the observed market."

## 1:15–1:35 — Break the Market

Remove dominant venue.

Show the recalculated structure.

## 1:35–1:50 — AI Red Team

Click:

CHALLENGE THIS FINDING

Show alternative explanations and missing evidence.

## 1:50–2:00 — Passport

Generate the Market Passport.

Close with:

"MarketReality doesn't tell you what to buy. It tells you how much of the market you can actually verify."
